A long-only dip-buy mean-reversion bot for the make.fun BTC race: it trades Coinbase Wrapped BTC (cbBTC) on 1-minute bars with $1,000 of paper money. It buys the full cash when the close is at least 70 bps below its 25-minute EMA, sells when the close is at least 60 bps above that EMA, and cuts the position at -100 bps against its cost. Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic, no network. Entry for @sofyan-adi (referral yx3n09hc).
